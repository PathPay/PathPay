# The PathPay Story

### The Problem (Our Origin)
- Founder tried buying a laptop using multiple apps (Bybit, Grey, Chipper, Redot, Plasma, Avicii) → failed every checkout.
- Time wasted, fees lost, uncertainty.
- Realization: P2P + stablecoins exist but **distribution to merchants is unreliable**.

### The Insight
- Easier to get stablecoins than USD in countries like Nigeria.
- Entry point: P2P onramping is already easy.
- Our job: take user USDT → ensure it reaches merchant reliably with minimal friction.

### The Mission
> Make global e-commerce frictionless for users outside privileged banking regions.

### Our Approach
1. **Short Term (Orchestrator)**:  
   - Aggregate likely failures → suggest best payment rails → minimize time & fees.
2. **Long Term (Neobank)**:  
   - Wallet + cards + automated routing + FX optimization → one-stop financial app.

### Why It Matters
- Hundreds of millions of users globally are stuck in the same problem.
- No product currently orchestrates payments intelligently for consumers in emerging markets.
