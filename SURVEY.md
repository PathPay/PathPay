# Payment Failure Collector – Survey Overview

**Google Form:** [Link](https://forms.gle/AV6PuvcGagaCBrV36)

## Purpose
- Understand where and why payments fail.
- Identify patterns for merchants, cards, and regions.
- Provide real data to train the orchestration intelligence engine.

## Key Metrics Collected
- Merchant Name & Category
- User Country
- Payment Method & Provider
- Success/Failure
- Error Messages
- Workarounds Tried
- Fees Paid
- Time Wasted
